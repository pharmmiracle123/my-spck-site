About Us

Richard’s Care Foundation is a humanitarian nonprofit organization committed to supporting individuals and communities with compassion, dignity, and purpose.

We believe that every life matters, and our work is guided by empathy and a genuine desire to make a difference where it is needed most. Through targeted support, community outreach, and advocacy, we respond to real needs and offer practical help that restores hope and improves lives.

Our mission goes beyond giving—we aim to uplift, protect, and empower people to face life with renewed strength and confidence. Every initiative we undertake is rooted in care, accountability, and the belief that humanity thrives when we stand together.

A Healthy Man, A Strong Community

Inside Richard’s Care Foundation Free Medical Outreach

The Story Behind It All

My name is Richard Thomas, and I am the humanitarian founder of Richard’s Care Foundation.

This project did not begin as a grand plan. It began with a question:

“How can I turn the love I receive into impact for others?”

In 2022, I was deeply celebrated by my church community. But I felt a conviction—this had to become something more.

So in 2023, I began a yearly mission of impact:

  • 2023: 12-day free leadership training
  • 2024: Financial empowerment training (NYSC experience)
  • 2025: Free mental health awareness program
---

The Moment That Changed Everything

Late in 2025, while visiting the Mechanic Village in Yenagoa, I met a man suffering from a severe foot ulcer.

He wasn’t ignoring treatment—he simply couldn’t afford it.

Despite not making sales that day, he still bought food on credit for his family.

That moment changed everything.

People are not neglecting their health—they are choosing survival.

---

From Referral to Responsibility

I had always referred people to hospitals. But then I asked:

“Why send them to the hospital… when I can bring the hospital to them?”

That question gave birth to this outreach—and to Richard’s Care Foundation.
